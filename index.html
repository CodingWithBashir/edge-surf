How to Play

Keyboard

  • ← / A Move left one lane
  • → / D Move right one lane
  • Space / ↑ / W Jump
  • ↓ / S Duck under hazards (hold)
  • P / Esc Pause
  • Enter Confirm / restart

Touch

  • Swipe ← → Change lane
  • Swipe ↑ Jump
  • Swipe ↓ Duck
  • Tap on-screen buttons Same actions
  • Pause icon Pause menu

Tips

  • Low hazards (coral, logs, whitewater) can be jumped. Tall hazards (rocks, buoys) must be steered around.
  • Overhead driftwood has to be ducked — hold Down as you pass beneath it.
  • Coins build your combo; a clean run keeps the score multiplier climbing.
  • Hitting a hazard costs a life. A Shield absorbs exactly one hit.
  • Every 1,200 m the Kraken wakes up. Outrun it — or lose a life to its tentacles.
